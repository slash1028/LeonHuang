<p class="intro-kicker">Multimodal affective intelligence</p>
<h1 class="intro-title">Understanding emotion<br>in human conversation.</h1>

<p class="intro-lead">I am a PhD student in Electrical and Electronic Engineering at <strong>The Hong Kong Polytechnic University</strong>, advised by Prof. Kong Aik Lee and Prof. Man-Wai Mak. I am also a visiting researcher at <strong>Kyoto University</strong> with Prof. Tatsuya Kawahara. My work builds conversational AI that can reason about emotion across language, speech and vision — with attention to personality, uncertainty and human preferences.</p>
